#### Schedule

请通过邮件预约具体时间。通常我会在工作日的上午或下午安排时间。

Please email me to arrange a specific time. I am generally available on weekday mornings or afternoons.

---

#### Location

请尽量提前1天预约具体地点，我也方便进行线上会议。

Please book a specific location at least one day in advance; online meetings are also welcome.

---

#### Meeting material

* 如讨论研究相关内容，请提前发送拟讨论工作的书面说明。
* 演示文稿或论文草稿均可。
* 若暂无上述材料，请发送一页纸的研究简介。
* 请于会面前一晚 20:00 前将材料发给我。

* If we are meeting to discuss research, please send me a written description of the work that you would like to discuss.
* Presentation slides or a paper draft are perfectly fine.
* If you do not have those, please send a one-page description of the research.
* Please send me the material by 8pm on the evening prior to our meeting.
